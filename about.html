His Story

The Journey

Born and raised in Nairobi, Micky Mimsanii discovered his gift for music at an early age — singing in church, school talent shows and local worship teams, before being led into his own path as a servant through music. His sound flows through gospel, contemporary worship and Afro-pop, all used as vessels to glorify God across cultures and generations.

His journey of faith is a testimony of God’s grace through every season. In moments of doubt, loss and breakthrough, it has been God who has sustained and shaped every song. What is created is not for performance, but as a testimony — music birthed in prayer, surrender and real encounters with Him.

"Every song I write is a conversation with God — I’m just a vessel chosen to share what He places in my heart."

— Micky Mimsanii

As a servant in ministry, Micky does not see himself as the center, but as a vessel. The assignment remains clear — to serve, to lead others into worship, and to point every heart back to God. Every platform, every moment, and every sound belongs to Him alone.

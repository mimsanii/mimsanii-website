Common Questions

Partnership FAQ

How quickly will you respond to my enquiry?
+
Quick contact messages are typically responded to within 48 hours. Full partnership enquiries may take up to 72 hours as they require the team to review your proposal properly. If your matter is urgent, please call or WhatsApp directly on +254 115 555 072.
Does Micky minister at small churches or only large events?
+
Micky serves all sizes of congregation — from intimate worship nights with 50 people to large gospel concerts with thousands. The size of the crowd does not determine the commitment to excellence and the heart behind the ministry. Every booking is treated with the same seriousness.
What is included in a church ministry booking?
+
A typical church booking includes worship leading, a gospel performance set, and where relevant, a short spoken ministry moment. Technical requirements (sound, lighting, backline) are discussed during the booking process. A formal rider and booking agreement is provided after initial discussions.
Can brands partner with Micky even if they are not faith-based?
+
Yes — brands do not need to be faith-based to partner with the ministry. However, all partnerships must align with the values of the ministry: respect for faith, family and community. Brands that conflict with these values (alcohol, gambling, adult content) will not be considered regardless of the financial offer.
How does a music collaboration work?
+
Music collaborations start with a creative conversation — sharing ideas, listening to existing work, and establishing whether there is genuine creative alignment. From there, a songwriting or feature session is arranged. All collaboration details including royalty splits and release timelines are agreed in writing before any studio time begins.
How do I get a press kit or media assets?
+
Press kits including hi-res artist photos, official bio, discography and streaming links are available on the About page. For specific media requests — interviews, editorial features or broadcast appearances — use the quick contact form above or email directly.
Is there a minimum sponsorship amount?
+
For individual donations, there is no minimum — every shilling counts. For formal sponsorship partnerships (with branding, perks and recognition), the Seed tier starts at KSh 5,000. See the Sponsorship Tiers on the Donate page for full details.
